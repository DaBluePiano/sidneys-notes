# Key Definitions and Formulas — Cambridge 11 Advanced Year 11

## Key Definitions

### Pronumeral

A variable symbol representing a number.

### Function

A rule assigning each input x in its domain to a single output f(x).

### Domain

The set of allowable input values for a function.

### Range

The set of attainable output values for a function.

### Derivative

Instantaneous rate of change; slope of tangent to curve.

### Expected Value

The theoretical mean of a random variable: $E(X)=\sum_x xP(X=x)$.

### Variance

$\mathrm{Var}(X)=E(X^2)-[E(X)]^2$, measures spread.

## Key Formulas

### Index Laws

$$
$a^m a^n = a^{m+n},\; \frac{a^m}{a^n}=a^{m-n},\; (a^m)^n=a^{mn},\; a^0=1$
$$

### Quadratic Formula

$$
$x=\dfrac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$
$$

### Distance Formula

$$
$d=\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}$
$$

### Midpoint

$$
$M\left(\dfrac{x_1+x_2}{2},\dfrac{y_1+y_2}{2}\right)$
$$

### Vertex of Quadratic

$$
$x=-\dfrac{b}{2a}$
$$

### Power Rule

$$
$\dfrac{d}{dx}x^n=nx^{n-1}$
$$

### Derivative Definition

$$
$f'(x)=\lim_{h\to0}\dfrac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}$
$$

### E Definition

$$
$e=\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(1+\dfrac{1}{n}\right)^n$
$$

### Log Definition

$$
$y=\log_a x \iff a^y=x$
$$

### Change of Base

$$
$\log_a x=\dfrac{\log_b x}{\log_b a}$
$$

### Sine Rule

$$
$\dfrac{a}{\sin A}=\dfrac{b}{\sin B}=\dfrac{c}{\sin C}$
$$

### Cosine Rule

$$
$c^2=a^2+b^2-2ab\cos C$
$$

### Pythagorean Trig

$$
$\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$
$$

### Expected Value

$$
$E(X)=\sum_x xP(X=x)$
$$

### Variance

$$
$\mathrm{Var}(X)=E(X^2)-[E(X)]^2$
$$

### Permutations

$$
$P(n,r)=\dfrac{n!}{(n-r)!}$
$$

### Combinations

$$
$\binom{n}{r}=\dfrac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}$
$$

### Arc Length

$$
$s=r\theta$ (radians)
$$

### Sector Area

$$
$A=\tfrac12 r^2\theta$ (radians)
$$
