## Key Formulas

For discrete random variable $X$ taking values $x_i$ with probabilities $p_i$:

$$
E(X)=\sum_i x_i p_i.
$$

Second moment:

$$
E(X^2)=\sum_i x_i^2 p_i.
$$

Variance:

$$
\mathrm{Var}(X)=E\big[(X-\mu)^2\big]=E(X^2)-[E(X)]^2.
$$

Standard deviation: $\sigma=\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}(X)}$.

### Interpretation

- $E(X)$ is the long-run average (theoretical mean).
- Variance measures spread; larger variance ⇒ more spread.

### Sampling

- Sampling involves computing statistics from experimental or observed samples; theoretical distributions give expected sample behaviour.
- Use relative frequencies as experimental estimates of probabilities.
