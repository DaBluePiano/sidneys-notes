# Chapter 7 — Exponential and Logarithmic Functions

> **Scope:** index laws (including fractional/negative), logarithms and their laws, graphs, and applications (growth/decay).

---

## Contents

- Index laws and fractional/negative indices
- Logarithm definition and change-of-base
- Log laws
- Graphs of exponentials and logs
- Applications: growth/decay, modelling

---

## Essentials

### Indices

$$
a^m a^n=a^{m+n},\qquad a^{-n}=\frac{1}{a^n},\qquad a^{m/n}=(a^{1/n})^m.
$$

### Logarithms

Definition: $y=\log_a x\iff a^y=x$ (domain $x>0$).

Change of base:

$$
\log_a x=\frac{\log_b x}{\log_b a}.
$$

### Log Laws

$$
\log_a(xy)=\log_a x+\log_a y,\quad
\log_a\left(\frac{x}{y}\right)=\log_a x-\log_a y,\quad
\log_a(x^k)=k\log_a x.
$$

### Graph Features

- $y=a^x$ has domain $\mathbb{R}$, range $y>0$; horizontal asymptote $y=0$.
- $y=\log_a x$ has domain $x>0$, range $\mathbb{R}$; vertical asymptote $x=0$.
- Exponential and log graphs are inverses (reflect across $y=x$).

### Applications

- Discrete/continuous growth models: $P(t)=P_0a^t$ or $P(t)=P_0e^{kt}$.
- Solve doubling/half-life problems with logs.
