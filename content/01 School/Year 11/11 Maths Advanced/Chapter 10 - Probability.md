## Essentials

### Basic Formula (uniform Sample space)

For $N$ equally likely outcomes and event $E$ with $|E|$ favourable outcomes,

$$
P(E)=\frac{|E|}{N}.
$$

### Addition Rule

$$
P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B).
$$

### Conditional Probability

$$
P(A\mid B)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)},\qquad P(A\cap B)=P(B)P(A\mid B).
$$

Use reduced sample-space reasoning when an event (like \"at least one\") is given.

### Counting Tools

- Permutations: $P(n,r)=\dfrac{n!}{(n-r)!}$.
- Combinations: $\binom{n}{r}=\dfrac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}$.

- Distinguish independence from mutually exclusive events (mutually exclusive $\Rightarrow$ not independent unless trivial).
