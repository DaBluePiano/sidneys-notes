## Core Formulas

Derivative (limit form):

$$
f'(x)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}.
$$

Power rule: $\dfrac{d}{dx}x^n=nx^{n-1}.$

Product rule: $(fg)'=f'g+fg'.$

Quotient rule: $\left(\frac{u}{v}\right)'=\frac{v u'-u v'}{v^2}.$

Chain rule: $\frac{d}{dx}f(g(x))=f'(g(x))\cdot g'(x).$

---

## Rates of Change & Interpretation

- Instantaneous rate = derivative.
- In kinematics, velocity $v=\dfrac{ds}{dt}$, acceleration $a=\dfrac{dv}{dt}=\dfrac{d^2s}{dt^2}$.

### Continuity & Differentiability

- Differentiability implies continuity; continuity does not guarantee differentiability (corners, cusps).

## Study Tips

- Memorise the differentiation rules and practise applying them in sequence for composites.
- For applied questions, translate units and interpret derivatives physically.
