# Chapter 4 — Transformations and Symmetry

> **Scope:** translations, reflections, scalings, symmetry (even/odd), absolute value and composite functions.

---

## Contents

- Translations and order of operations
- Reflections and stretches/compressions
- Even & odd functions
- Absolute value functions (piecewise)
- Composite functions $f\circ g$

---

## Core Rules & Examples

### Transformations

- Horizontal shift: $y=f(x-h)$ shifts right by $h$.
- Vertical shift: $y=f(x)+k$ shifts up by $k$.
- Vertical stretch/compression: $y=a\,f(x)$ (stretch if $|a|>1$).
- Horizontal stretch/compression: $y=f(bx)$ (shrink if $|b|>1$).
- Reflections: $y=-f(x)$ (x-axis), $y=f(-x)$ (y-axis).

**Order:** horizontal transforms inside $f(\cdot)$ first, then stretches/reflections, then vertical translations.

### Symmetry

- Even: $f(-x)=f(x)$ (y-axis symmetry).
- Odd: $f(-x)=-f(x)$ (origin symmetry).

### Absolute Value

- $|x|=\begin{cases}x,&x\ge0\\-x,&x<0\end{cases}$.
- Analyze $|f(x)|$ piecewise or reflect negative parts of $f$.

### Composite Functions

- $(f\circ g)(x)=f(g(x))$. Domain requires $x$ in domain of $g$ and $g(x)$ in domain of $f$.

---

## Study Tips

- When graphing multiple transforms, apply them stepwise to a known base graph.
- Test symmetry quickly by evaluating $f(-x)$.
