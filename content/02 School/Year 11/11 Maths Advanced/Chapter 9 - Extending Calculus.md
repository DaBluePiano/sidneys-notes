# Chapter 9 — Extending Calculus

> **Scope:** the natural base $e$, differentiation of exponentials/logarithms, radian measure and trig graphs in radians.

---

---

## Essentials

### Euler's Number $e$

$$
e=\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(1+\frac{1}{n}\right)^n\approx2.71828.
$$

Natural exponential has special derivative:

$$
\frac{d}{dx}e^x=e^x.
$$

General exponential:

$$
\frac{d}{dx}a^x=a^x\ln a.
$$

Logarithm:

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\ln x=\frac{1}{x}\quad(x>0).
$$

### Radian Measure

- By definition $ \theta\ (\text{radians})=\dfrac{\text{arc length}}{r}$.
- Arc length: $s=r\theta$. Sector area: $\tfrac12 r^2\theta$.

### Trig in Radians

- Differentiation of trig functions is simplest in radians (e.g. $\dfrac{d}{dx}\sin x=\cos x$).
