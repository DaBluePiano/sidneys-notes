# Chapter 3 — Functions and Graphs

> **Scope:** functions, domain/range, key graph families and transformations, asymptotes and inverses.

---

## Key Points

### Functions

- A function $f$ maps each $x$ in domain $D$ to a single value $f(x)$.
- Notation: $f:D\to\mathbb{R},\ y=f(x)$.

### Domain & Range

- Domain: allowable $x$-values (watch denominators and even roots).
- Range: set of achievable $y$-values.

### Quadratics

- Standard form: $y=ax^2+bx+c$ with vertex at $x=-\dfrac{b}{2a}$.
- Vertex (vertex form): $y=a(x-h)^2+k$ where $(h,k)$ is the vertex.

### Polynomial Behaviour

- End behaviour determined by degree and leading coefficient.
- Roots and multiplicities: an odd multiplicity crosses the axis, even multiplicity touches.

### Rational Functions

- $f(x)=\dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}$. Vertical asymptotes at roots of $Q$ unless factor cancels (hole).
- Horizontal asymptote: compare degrees $n=\deg P$, $m=\deg Q$:
  - $n<m\Rightarrow y=0$;
  - $n=m\Rightarrow y=\dfrac{\text{lead}(P)}{\text{lead}(Q)}$;
  - $n>m\Rightarrow$ oblique asymptote via division.

### Inverses

- $f^{-1}$ exists only if $f$ is one-to-one.
- Graph of $f^{-1}$ is reflection of graph of $f$ in line $y=x$.

## Quick Study Checklist

- For each graph: identify domain, range, intercepts, asymptotes, and end behaviour.
- Practice sketching by applying transformations to known base graphs.
