## Essentials

### Basic Definitions (right triangle)

$$
\sin\theta=\frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}},\quad
\cos\theta=\frac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}},\quad
\tan\theta=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}.
$$

### Unit Circle

- For angle $\theta$, coordinates $(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$.
- Standard angles to memorise: $0,\ \tfrac{\pi}{6},\ \tfrac{\pi}{4},\ \tfrac{\pi}{3},\ \tfrac{\pi}{2}$.

### Key Identities

$$
\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1,\quad
\sin(\alpha\pm\beta)=\sin\alpha\cos\beta\pm\cos\alpha\sin\beta,
$$
$$
\cos(\alpha\pm\beta)=\cos\alpha\cos\beta\mp\sin\alpha\sin\beta.
$$

### Trig Equations

- Use identities to reduce to basic forms; include general solutions with periodicity $2\pi k$ (or $360^\circ$ variants).

### Sine & Cosine Rules

- Sine rule:
$$
\frac{a}{\sin A}=\frac{b}{\sin B}=\frac{c}{\sin C}.
$$
- Cosine rule:
$$
c^2=a^2+b^2-2ab\cos C.
$$
- Area of triangle:
$$
\text{Area}=\tfrac12 ab\sin C.
$$

## Study Tips

- Use reference angles and quadrant signs when solving.
- Keep exact values (roots and fractions of $\pi$) rather than decimals where possible.
