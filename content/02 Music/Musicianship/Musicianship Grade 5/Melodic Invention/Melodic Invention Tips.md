In the exam, a couplet is given and you must set the couplet to a melody of eight bars of 3/4 or four bars of 6/8, choosing your own rhythmic scheme and writing the words below the notes.

1. Place vertical lines in the couplet in front of the accent words or syllables, writing 8 lines to form 8 bars or 4 lines to form 4 bars.
2. Divide 2 staves into 4 bars each and write the words under the saves, making suer you hyphenate the words with more than one syllable.
3. Above the staff, compose a rhythmic pattern to *suit the words*. VARY your rhythmic pattern. Make sure that the words suite the rhythm you have composed. EACH SYLLABLE requires a separate note. If you write 2 notes to a syllable or word, place a slur over the 2 notes. Always watch the anacrusis value.
4. Compose a melody according to your rhythmic pattern. Try to follow these 12 suggestions;
	1. Anacrusis on the 5th note
	2. 1st note of 1st bar, tonic note
	3. 1st bar mostly of I harmony.
	4. 2nd bar, IV or vi harmony notes.
	5. 3rd bar, chord ii notes.
	6. End of 1st phrase, 5th note (Imperfect Cadence II-V).
	7. Bar 5 and 6 - Sequence, LN!
	8. Last note tonic note.
	9. 2nd Last Note - Note abover tonic (Perfect Cadence V-I).
	10. Watch LN at all times, cover it - 6 (7) 8 or 8 (7) 6.
	11. Place 2 slurs, 1 over each phrase.
	12. Italian word above the time sign and add expression marks.
The leading notes requires care in melody writing - don't leap to or from it, except in moving to other notes of chord V.

Remember this simple rule - protect the leading note on either side. Use 6 (7) 8 or 8 (7) 6, i.e., use it as part of a scale passage, either ascending descending.

Don't ever leap from the 4th degree to the 7th degree. An augmented 4th interval results and am elody must now have augmented intervals.

Try to reach a climactic point near the end.

Write in curves rather than jagged lines. Avoid repeated notes which tend to hold up the flow of the music. IT should be a pleasant-sounding tune and simple to harmonise.
