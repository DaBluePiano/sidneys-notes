Good afternoon, I'm Sidney and I will be performing *Toccata* from *Pour le Piano* by Claude Debussy.

Debussy was a French composer at the turn of the 20th century who helped move music away from the heavy, Romantic style into something more colorful and atmospheric. He’s often called an ‘Impressionist,’ though he disliked it.

This *Toccata*, the last movement of his suite *Pour le piano*, is about lightness, clarity and control. The texture is full of rapid, glittering melodies that shift constantly, and it being a toccata, really tests a pianist’s touch and articulation as everything has to be clear and sparkling.
